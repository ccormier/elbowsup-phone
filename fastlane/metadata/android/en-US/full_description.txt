Elbows Up is a fork of Fossify Phone with two additions:

• Caller ID names on incoming calls. It shows the name the carrier sends with a call: a business name, a caller's name, or a carrier-provided label such as "Likely Spam".
• A built-in call blocker. Write an ordered list of rules; calls that match are rejected, silenced, or answered and hung up. Rules can match numbers, hidden callers, caller ID names, or the lack of a name, and can be limited to schedules. A pause switch and a list of blocked calls are included.

Everything else — the dialer, contacts and call history — comes from Fossify Phone, and the fork keeps tracking upstream releases. No ads, no tracking, and no internet permission.

Elbows Up is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Fossify project. Fossify Phone and Fossify Commons are the upstream projects this fork is built on.
