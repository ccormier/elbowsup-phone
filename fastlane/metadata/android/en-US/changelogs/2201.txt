First Elbows Up release, based on Fossify Phone 1.11.1.

- Shows the caller ID name the carrier sends on incoming calls, for example "Likely Spam"
- Adds a rule-based call blocker: ordered rules, caller ID name matching, schedules, pauses, and a list of blocked calls
- Keeps everything from Fossify Phone, including the dialer, contacts and call history

No ads, no tracking, no internet permission.
